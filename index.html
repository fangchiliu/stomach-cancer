Overview

Why Gastric Adenocarcinoma?

🎯

Clinical Motivation

Gastric adenocarcinoma (STAD) is the fifth most common malignancy worldwide, with a 5-year survival rate below 35% for stage III–IV disease. Despite recent advances in HER2-targeted and checkpoint immunotherapy, a substantial fraction of patients lack actionable molecular targets — underscoring the need for deeper transcriptomic characterization.

🧬

Scientific Approach

This project applies a multi-resolution transcriptomic framework: bulk RNA-seq from TCGA-STAD provides population-level differential expression and survival associations, while upcoming single-cell analysis from CZ CELLxGENE will resolve cell-type-specific expression programs and tumor microenvironment heterogeneity.

📊

Analytical Scope

Analyses span differential expression (DESeq2), Kaplan-Meier survival modeling, gene set enrichment analysis (GSEA) against Hallmark and KEGG gene sets, and cell-type deconvolution. All code, processed data, and figures are tracked in version-controlled repositories.
