Computational Pipeline

Methods & Analytical Framework

All analyses were performed in R 4.3 and Python 3.11. Source code and reproducible workflows are available in the linked GitHub repository.

Pipeline Overview

End-to-End Workflow

1

Data Acquisition & Access

TCGA-STAD RNA-seq count matrices and clinical metadata were downloaded via the NCI Genomic Data Commons (GDC) Data Portal using the TCGAbiolinks R package (Colaprico et al. 2016). Data were retrieved for 375 primary tumor samples (HTSeq-Counts, GRCh38/hg38 reference genome, STAR 2-pass alignment). Matched adjacent normal tissue samples (N=30) were included for tumor-versus-normal contrast analyses.

TCGAbiolinks GDC Portal GRCh38 HTSeq-Counts
2

Quality Control & Preprocessing

Raw count matrices were assessed for outlier samples using principal component analysis and hierarchical clustering. Samples with fewer than 5×106 mapped reads, a ribosomal RNA contamination rate above 20%, or a 3′/5′ bias coefficient exceeding 0.6 were flagged for exclusion. Library size normalization was performed using DESeq2's median-of-ratios method. Low-expressed genes (fewer than 10 counts in at least 10 samples) were filtered prior to downstream analysis, retaining 18,342 of 60,483 annotated features.

DESeq2 edgeR MultiQC RSeQC PCA
3

Differential Expression Analysis

Differential gene expression between primary tumor (N=375) and adjacent normal tissue (N=30) was estimated using DESeq2 (Love et al. 2014) with the Wald test statistic. Covariates included in the design formula were: tissue type (tumor vs. normal), tumor purity (ESTIMATE algorithm), patient age, and sex. Genes were called differentially expressed at an adjusted p-value threshold of FDR < 0.05 (Benjamini-Hochberg correction) and a minimum absolute log₂ fold change of 1.5. Volcano plots and MA plots were generated using EnhancedVolcano and ggplot2.

DESeq2 ggplot2 EnhancedVolcano ESTIMATE R 4.3
4

Survival Analysis

Overall survival (OS) and progression-free survival (PFS) associations were evaluated using Kaplan-Meier estimator with log-rank testing. Patients were stratified into high/low expression groups at the median expression quantile for each gene of interest. Cox proportional hazards models adjusted for tumor stage (AJCC), age, and molecular subtype (EBV, MSI, CIN, GS) were used to derive multivariate hazard ratios. All survival analyses were conducted using the survival and survminer R packages.

survival survminer Kaplan-Meier Cox PH log-rank
5

Pathway Analysis (GSEA & ORA)

Gene Set Enrichment Analysis (GSEA; Subramanian et al. 2005) was performed using the fgsea R package with genes pre-ranked by the DESeq2 Wald statistic. Gene sets were sourced from MSigDB: Hallmark (H), KEGG (C2), and Gene Ontology Biological Process (C5) collections. Significance was defined as FDR < 0.25 (standard GSEA threshold) with a minimum gene set size of 15 and maximum of 500. Over-representation analysis (ORA) was additionally performed with clusterProfiler on the top 500 up- and down-regulated genes (by adjusted p-value).

fgsea clusterProfiler MSigDB KEGG GO:BP Hallmark

Statistical Considerations

Rigor & Reproducibility Notes

Multiple Testing

All p-values from differential expression testing are corrected using the Benjamini-Hochberg procedure. A genome-wide FDR threshold of 5% is applied for DEG calls; GSEA uses the standard 25% FDR threshold.

Tumor Purity

Tumor purity estimates from the ESTIMATE algorithm (Yoshihara et al. 2013) are included as a continuous covariate in the DESeq2 design formula to reduce confounding from stromal and immune cell contamination in bulk samples.

Batch Effects

TCGA sequencing plate and tissue source site are evaluated as potential batch covariates using sva (ComBat-seq). PCA plots are inspected before and after correction to confirm successful mitigation of technical variance.
